# CareerNetwork 🎯

CareerNetwork is a full-stack job networking platform that bridges the gap between **students** seeking opportunities and **recruiters** looking for potential candidates. It allows job seekers to apply, track their applications, and build profiles, while recruiters can post jobs, manage applications, and hire efficiently.

## 🔥 Features

### 👨‍🎓 Student Functionality:
- 🔍 **Search Jobs** — Browse through available job listings.
- 🎯 **Filter Jobs** — Filter listings based on:
  - Location
  - Job Profession
- 📄 **Apply for Jobs** — Submit applications with uploaded resumes.
- 🧑‍💻 **Profile Management** — Update:
  - Skills
  - Resume (PDF)
- 📊 **Track Application Status** — Monitor whether job applications are:
  - ✅ Accepted
  - ❌ Rejected
  - ⏳ Pending

---

### 🧑‍💼 Recruiter Functionality:
- 🏢 **Register Company** — Create and manage company profiles.
- 📢 **Post Multiple Jobs** — Add job listings under a registered company.
- 🔍 **View Applicants** — Track candidates who apply to job postings.
- 📂 **Review Resumes** — Evaluate applicant resumes and update their status (Accept/Reject).
- 📝 **Edit Jobs & Company Info** — Make real-time changes to job descriptions and company profiles.
